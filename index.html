Spacing is the gap between neighbouring holes. Lines along the stroke put an even row of stones around each letter, then run lines of stones through the middle of each stroke (more than one where it is wide enough), offset to sit in the gaps. Nested fill sits each next row in the gaps of the row outside it. Inner stones can be a different size from the outline row (for example SS10 outline, SS8 inside). Inset moves stones in from the letter edge (negative lets them overhang). Thicken fattens or thins the font.

Cricut Design Space: Upload → SVG. The size is kept. Select everything and Attach so the holes cut in place, then cut on template material (sticky flock or flock) with the setting for that material.

Silhouette Studio: Designer Edition opens the SVG. Basic Edition: use the DXF and check the size after import.

Small holes (SS6 and below) can tear flock. Slow the blade, use 2 passes, or step up to SS10.

Editing: turn on Edit stones above the preview, then click a stone to remove it or click an empty spot to add one. Ctrl+Z undoes.

View: drag to pan, wheel or +/- to zoom, 0 to fit. On a touch screen, drag with one finger and pinch with two.

Templates don't need mirroring: stones are brushed in face up and lifted with transfer tape. Mirror only for special cases.

Edit mode: tap a stone to remove it, tap empty space to add one.

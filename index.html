Next: open the file in your cutter software, cut the holes from rhinestone template material (flock), brush the stones in, then lift them with transfer tape and press them onto the shirt.

1. Design: type your text at the top, then pick a font under Text, the finished size under Size and the stone size you own under Rhinestones. The box above the preview shows how many stones to buy.

2. Download the cutting file under Export: SVG for Cricut Design Space, DXF for Silhouette Studio Basic Edition.

Cricut Design Space: Upload → SVG. The size is kept. Select everything and Attach so the holes cut in place, then cut on template material (sticky flock or flock) with the setting for that material.

Silhouette Studio: Designer Edition opens the SVG. Basic Edition: use the DXF and check the size after import.

3. Cut and fill: cut the holes from the template material and weed them out. Brush the stones over the template until every hole holds one, face up. Lift them with transfer tape, lay the tape on the shirt and press as the stone maker says.

Templates don't need mirroring: stones are brushed in face up and lifted with transfer tape. Mirror only for special cases.

Stone sizes: SS is the size number on the stone packet; the stone size list gives each one in mm. Small holes (SS6 and below) can tear flock. Slow the blade, use 2 passes, or step up to SS10.

Editing: turn on Edit stones above the preview, then click a stone to remove it or click an empty spot to add one. Ctrl+Z or the ↶ button undoes. Finish the text, size and stones first: changing them clears the edits.

View: drag to pan, wheel or +/- to zoom, 0 to fit. On a touch screen, drag with one finger and pinch with two.

Saving: the design is kept in this browser, so it is still here when you come back. Copy link gives a link that opens the same design anywhere: keep it or send it to someone.

Edit mode: click a stone to remove it, click empty space to add one.
